# Bundled sound effects: license

The `.wav` files in this folder (Whoosh, Whoosh (fast), Hit / boom, Riser) were synthesised for
Ligxt Video Editor by `scripts/make_sfx.py` from noise and sine waves. They contain no recorded or
third-party material and are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Each `.json` sidecar records `peak`: the moment (seconds) that should land on a cut, used when the
editor places effects on transitions.
